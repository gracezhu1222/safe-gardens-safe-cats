Research made readable.

Our manuscript describes why the team began this project, how it conducted a community survey and field visit, the April 2026 veterinarian interview, an exploratory SD-CPRI idea, profiles of 15 studied plants, and practical safety information.

Status: Draft under scientific and editorial review. The complete book is not yet posted for download.

We are checking image permissions, scientific names, citations, and final authorship credits before distributing the book publicly.

15 research entries plus advice on identifying plants and contacting professionals.

Safety first

This website shares educational information, not veterinary diagnoses or treatment. If you suspect exposure to a hazardous plant, contact a veterinarian or animal poison-control service promptly.
