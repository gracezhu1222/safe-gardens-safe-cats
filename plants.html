Plant guide · Based on our 2026 research

Explore the plants.

Browse the 15 plants in our student book, including rosemary as an explicit non-toxic comparison. These are educational summaries, not an official risk ranking or diagnosis tool.

Before using this guide: Check plant scientific names, consult current veterinary references, and contact a veterinarian promptly after a possible exposure. The project categories are preliminary teaching labels, not clinically validated severity ratings. Photo placeholders indicate images awaiting licensing review.

Survey note: “At home” and “aware” counts come from 114 survey responses collected through convenience sampling. They do not represent citywide prevalence or poisoning incidence. We have not published a validated SD-CPRI rank.

Source and identification notes

This guide adapts descriptions from the team's draft manuscript and should be rechecked before publication. Specific exposure questions belong with veterinary professionals. For independent reference, consult the ASPCA cats plant list ↗ and Cornell's small animal toxins guidance ↗.
